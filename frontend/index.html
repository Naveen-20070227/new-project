Who Are We?

Yojana.ai is a technology-driven digital platform focused on making government opportunities more accessible through intelligent and user-centered technology. It is built with the vision of creating a simpler, more inclusive, and transparent digital experience for beneficiaries navigating government-supported financial opportunities.

Services

01

Scheme Eligibility Checker

Check your eligibility for government-supported loan schemes based on your personal, financial, and business details, and identify the schemes that best match your requirements.

02

EMI Calculator

Calculate your estimated monthly EMI based on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure, helping you understand your potential repayment commitment.

03

Channelized Agency Locator

Find suitable channelized agencies and lending partners based on your location, making it easier to connect with the right agency for your loan application.

Key Features

Multilingual

Access the platform in multiple languages, making government-supported financial opportunities easier to understand and access.

Reason for Eligibility

Get clear, understandable explanations of why you are eligible or not eligible for a particular scheme, powered by an LLM.

Intelligent Channel Partner Mapping

Find the best-suited channel partner, not simply the nearest one, based on your requirements, eligibility, scheme, and location.

Voice Access

Interact with the platform using voice, making the system more accessible and easier to use for beneficiaries.

Easy One-Click Service

Access essential services quickly with a simple and user-friendly one-click experience, reducing unnecessary steps.

Transparency

Create greater transparency between the beneficiary and channel partner by providing clear information about eligibility, schemes, and the loan process.
